# Joining Origins Rebirth

Follow these steps to join us:

---

## **Java Edition (Windows, Mac)**

1. **Launch Minecraft**  
 Open the Minecraft Launcher and ensure you are signed in with your Mojang or Microsoft account.
2. **Switch to Correct Version**  
 In the launcher, ensure you've selected the appropriate game version:
  - Click **Installations** &gt; **New Installation**.
  - Select the vanilla Minecraft version the server uses.
  - Save and launch the game from this profile.
3. **Click Multiplayer**  
 On the main menu, click **Multiplayer**.
4. **Add Server**  
 Click **Add Server** and enter:
  - **Server Name**: *Choose a name, e.g., "Origins Rebirth"*
  - **Server Address**: `mc.originsrebirth.com`
5. **Save and Connect**  
 Click **Done**, select the server, and press **Join Server**.
6. **Enjoy the Game**  
 Once connected, explore the beautiful Terralith world!

---

## **Bedrock Edition** (Windows 10/11, Mobile)

1. **Launch Minecraft Bedrock**  
 Open Minecraft on your device and ensure you’re signed in with your Microsoft account.
2. **Access the Servers Tab**
  - **Windows 10/11, iOS, Android**: Go to **Play** &gt; **Servers**.
  - **Consoles (Xbox/PlayStation/Switch)**: The "Servers" tab may require DNS adjustments (see note below).
3. **Add the Server**
  - Scroll to the bottom of the server list and tap **Add Server**.
  - Enter these details:
    - **Server Name**: *e.g., "Origins Rebirth"*
    - **Server Address**: `mc.originsrebirth.com`
    - **Port**: `19132`
4. **Save and Connect**  
 Save the server, select it from the list, and click **Join Server**.
5. **Enjoy the Game**  
 Once loaded, start playing!

## Bedrock Edition (PlayStation, Nintendo Switch)

Due to restrictions on console platforms, players cannot natively enter custom IP addresses. To connect to Origins Rebirth on PlayStation or Nintendo Switch, follow these steps:

1. **Change DNS Settings:**
  - (PlayStation) Go to your console's **Network Settings** and select **Set up internet connection**, In **Advance Settings**, set the **DNS settings** to **Manual**. Change your **Primary DNS** to `45.55.68.52` (provided by BedrockConnect) and **Secondary DNS** to `8.8.8.8`.
  - (Nintendo Switch) Go to your console's **Inernet Settings** and set the **Primary DNS** to `104.238.130.180` (provided by BedrockConnect) and the Secondary DNS to `8.8.8.8`.
2. **Launch Minecraft:** Open the game and navigate to the "Servers" tab.
3. **Select a Featured Server:** Join any of the featured servers. A menu will then pop up allowing you to input a custom Server IP and Port.
4. **Enter Details:** Input `mc.originsrebirth.com` and port `19132` to connect.
